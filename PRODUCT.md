# Product

## Register

product

## Users

CSUF students deciding what to eat at the campus dining hall, often between classes and using one hand in bright outdoor or dining-hall light. They need to see the current menu quickly, understand source-provided nutrition, assemble a feasible plate for a dietary goal, and optionally keep a simple meal diary.

## Product Purpose

GSpot Eats turns the current dining-hall menu into a trustworthy menu browser, macro tracker, and practical plate planner. Success means a student can choose a meal period, find relevant stations, build or accept a realistic plate, and understand its approximate calories and macros without scanning the provider website or doing arithmetic.

## Brand Personality

Clear, energetic, and trustworthy. The voice is concise and helpful without sounding institutional, clinical, or judgmental.

## Anti-references

- Not an official CSUF, Chartwells, Compass Group, or Dine On Campus product.
- Not a bodybuilding dashboard full of rings, streak pressure, or moral labels for food.
- Not a medical nutrition or allergy-safety tool.
- Not a generic card-grid SaaS interface, glassmorphic demo, or school-branded clone.

## Design Principles

1. Menu truth before recommendation: always show source, meal period, and freshness.
2. Decisions before dashboards: lead with what is available and what to take.
3. Explain every recommendation using visible nutrition or source labels.
4. Preserve uncertainty: never invent servings, ingredients, allergens, or nutrition.
5. Stay independent: use original branding and state non-affiliation plainly.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA where applicable to mobile interfaces. Maintain 44-point touch targets, readable contrast in bright light, screen-reader labels and states, dynamic text tolerance, non-color-only status communication, and reduced-motion-safe interactions. Dietary and allergen labels reproduce source information and never claim medical safety.
