# GSpot Eats Privacy Policy — Draft

GSpot Eats is an independent dining-hall menu and nutrition-planning application. This draft must be reviewed, dated, and hosted at a public URL before release.

## Data stored on the device

Meal diary entries, nutrition goals, and the default plate direction are stored locally on the user’s device. Version 0.1 does not require an account and does not intentionally upload diary contents or nutrition goals.

## Menu data

The app downloads normalized menu and nutrition snapshots from the GSpot Eats service. Server logs may contain operational request information such as time, response status, and service diagnostics, but must not include diary entries or personal nutrition goals.

## Analytics and advertising

Version 0.1 contains no advertising SDK and no behavioral analytics SDK. This section must be updated before adding either.

## Retention and deletion

Users can delete all locally stored diary entries from the Diary screen. Removing the app also removes its local data, subject to operating-system backup behavior.
